# Moderation and appeals

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

One administrator operates the beta. Reports go through the Report action on a post, comment, profile or community, or to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}, which is how visitors without accounts report. Give the URL, what happened and any immediate danger. Do not attach illegal or intimate imagery.

## How decisions are made

We consider reports, relevant context, our rules and applicable law. Automated controls may quarantine a hash match, reject blocked peers or hold suspected spam. Human review checks uncertainty and contested decisions. A match is a lead, not proof that a person committed a crime.

We may warn, label permitted sensitive content, restrict visibility, remove content, limit posting, suspend an account or stop federation with a server. Urgent danger can justify immediate restriction before review. We apply restrictions consistently and proportionately, considering legitimate expression and privacy. Pornography, exploitation and terrorist promotion are not permitted. Lawful discussion of these subjects is assessed in context.

When we restrict content or an account, we provide the action, duration and scope, the relevant facts, the rule or legal ground, any automated involvement and how to appeal. Where applicable, this statement accompanies the restriction. Reporter identity and harmful evidence are not normally disclosed. A specific legal prohibition on notification is recorded and reviewed. [DSA Art 17](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065).

## Timelines

| Matter                                            | How we handle it                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Immediate danger or apparent child abuse material | Urgent triage and restriction; prompt qualifying authority report.                                                |
| Valid intimate-image request                      | As soon as possible and within 48 hours; target 24 hours. Known copies are included.                              |
| eSafety removal notice                            | By the notice deadline, ordinarily 24 hours; internal target 12 hours.                                            |
| Other illegal-content notice                      | Acknowledge without undue delay; target a decision within 72 hours, sooner when severity requires.                |
| Ordinary appeal                                   | Aim to acknowledge within 2 days and decide within 7 days. If delayed, explain why and give the next update date. |

The 72-hour and seven-day figures are service targets, not extensions of legal deadlines. Relevant legal clocks: [TAKE IT DOWN s3](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ12/html/PLAW-119publ12.htm), [UK OSA s10](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10/data.html), [Australian Online Safety Act ss65, 77, 88, 109](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2021A00076/latest/text). EU terrorist-content orders can require action within one hour [TCO Art 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32021R0784).

## Ask for review

Reply to the decision or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with “Appeal”, the case number, the decision you dispute and your explanation. Appeals are free; you can appeal without an active account. We accept appeals for at least six months after a decision and consider later requests where reasonable. This is our chosen policy while relying on DSA small-service relief, not a claim that all DSA platform duties apply. [DSA Arts 19–20](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065).

During the single-admin beta the same administrator reconsiders the case, reviews the original grounds and records fresh reasons. We cannot promise an independent internal reviewer. We do not penalise good-faith appeals. A successful appeal restores the content and access we still hold. Removed content is kept hidden for six months after the decision, matching the appeal window, and then purged unless a legal hold applies, under the moderation row of our [retention schedule](/privacy); the decision states which row applies. An appeal that succeeds within those six months can restore the content, however late in the window it is made; after the purge a later request cannot bring it back. We also correct relevant records. A lawful preservation or another valid restriction may remain, with an explanation.

You may use available courts, report matters within eSafety's remit through [eSafety](https://www.esafety.gov.au/report), or complain about DSA compliance to the relevant Digital Services Coordinator where that right applies [DSA Art 53](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065). Copyright counter-notices have a separate [procedure](/dmca). We do not promise statutory out-of-court dispute settlement where an exemption applies.
