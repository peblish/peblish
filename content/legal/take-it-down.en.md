# Remove an intimate image

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

If an intimate image of you was shared on feddit.com without your consent, request its removal with the Report action on the post, or by email to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You do not need an account to email us. Someone you authorise may act for you. This includes realistic digitally altered or AI-generated intimate depictions. Threats to share images can also be reported.

## What to send

Send a written request with:

1. Your physical or electronic signature, or your authorised representative's signature. Typing your name can serve as an electronic signature.
2. Enough information to identify and locate the depiction, such as the post or media URL. You do not have to find every copy.
3. A short statement that you believe in good faith it was published without your consent, with any useful context. If acting for someone else, state your authority.
4. A way to contact you about the request.

These are the US statutory request elements [TAKE IT DOWN Act s3(a)(1)(B)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ12/html/PLAW-119publ12.htm). Do not send the image again, a government identity document, or a police report. We may ask a narrow question if necessary to locate the material or confirm authority, but do not require these by default.

## What happens next

We acknowledge receipt with a case number. We remove qualifying content as soon as possible and no later than 48 hours after receiving a valid request, making reasonable efforts within that time to find and remove known identical copies on our service. Our internal target is 24 hours. [TAKE IT DOWN Act s3(a)(3)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ12/html/PLAW-119publ12.htm).

For qualifying UK intimate-image reports, the process also addresses material identified as the same or substantially the same, as soon as reasonably practicable and within 48 hours of the report. [UK OSA s10(3A)–(3B)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/10/data.html). We triage all intimate-image reports urgently even if a request needs clarification, and do not wait for an appeal or a police investigation before taking protective action.

Removal covers the post, local media and derivatives, proxy access and cached copies we control. We block known reappearances where our tools allow. A remote original may remain on another service; we can help identify its reporting route without sending your identity to an unsafe operator.

We tell you what we did, or explain why the request did not qualify and how to ask for review at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. A review does not suspend a required removal. Your identity is restricted; necessary legal disclosures may still occur. We do not retain an intimate image just to keep a complaint history.

If the depicted person was under 18 when the image was made, tell us if you safely can; the urgent child-safety process also applies. You may use [NCMEC Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) or, for adult imagery, [StopNCII](https://stopncii.org/) for additional help. feddit.com is not part of either matching network. These tools are separate from your request to us. [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-take-it-down-act).
