## Where information goes

The application and database run on the operator's Easypanel server hosted in Canada by technocloud.ca. Cloudflare provides network protection and delivery. Planned local-upload storage is Cloudflare R2; storage, backup and processing locations are listed below. Account mail provider and location: Purelymail (Add Rabbit LLC), United States, with servers in Amazon Web Services' northern Virginia region. Backup location: Cloudflare R2 object storage (Cloudflare, Inc., United States), with North America requested as the storage region. Active media-safety processors and what each receives: Cloudflare's CSAM Scanning Tool, which checks the files Cloudflare caches for feddit.com. No other media-safety processor is active, and image uploads are off.

After cutover the previous PieFed instance on box.feddit.com is kept stopped and restorable for 30 days, and its archive is kept offline on the operator's own computer in Australia until {{PIEFED_ARCHIVE_DELETE_DATE}}, which is the cutover date plus 30 days. On that date both the instance and the archive are deleted unless a legal hold applies. Backups taken before cutover follow the ordinary backup row in the retention schedule. Actor signing keys are kept only as long as the federation identity needs them.

ActivityPub sends public posts, profiles, comments and interaction events to independent servers around the world. Those operators decide how they process their copies and are not simply our contracted processors. Remote public content is cached locally as database objects. Deletion requests are sent to peers, but we cannot make every peer erase its copy.

All remote media, including link-preview thumbnails, is streamed at view time through our signed non-persisting proxy, not hotlinked or mirrored. It hides the visitor's IP from the remote media host during that fetch. It does not make visits anonymous: feddit.com and Cloudflare process connection information, and opening an external link exposes you to that site's practices. The proxy must not save response bodies to disk, logs or object storage. An image may be held in Cloudflare's edge cache for up to one hour, so it loads faster and the remote host is asked less often, if, when it was fetched, the content it belongs to could be seen by anyone without signing in. When that content or its source is taken down, we ask Cloudflare to remove the image, normally within about 20 seconds, and ask again a minute later; if removal fails, the image can stay in that cache until its hour is up. Images of content that only some viewers could see when they were fetched, such as sensitive or removed content, are not put in a CDN cache. Short-lived request buffers and signed URLs are not a durable media cache.

Link-preview thumbnails are not stored. They are fetched through the same proxy when a post is viewed and kept only as the edge cache above allows. Nothing else fetched from remote servers is stored until our safety scanning is live; that scanning applies before publication to local uploads and, later, to any stored copies. Local uploads, avatars and other stored derivatives stay disabled if that scan path is unavailable.

International processing and federation require assessment under GDPR Chapter V where applicable. Canada hosting alone does not establish a valid transfer mechanism for all recipients. Countries involved: Australia, where the operator is; Canada, where the server is hosted; and the United States, where Cloudflare, Inc. (network and backup storage) and the mail provider are based, with Cloudflare's network handling traffic worldwide. [GDPR Arts 13–14, 44–49](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679).

Legal holds are narrow, access-limited and reviewed, with a reason, scope and expiry. They do not leave removed content public. Intimate images are not retained for 24 months merely because a report exists. We retain only necessary incident records unless a specific preservation basis requires the material.

## Your choices and rights

Contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} for access, correction, a copy/export, deletion, restriction or objection. Where applicable, you can withdraw consent and request portability of qualifying data. We verify identity proportionately and do not require an identity document by default. Exports exclude other people's private information and illegal material. You may complain to your relevant supervisory authority and seek a judicial remedy. [GDPR Arts 12, 15–22, 77–79](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679). We offer the same request route to everyone.

Australia's Privacy Act may exempt a small business, but turnover is not the only test. [OAIC small-business guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/small-business). We follow this policy whether or not the Privacy Act applies to the operator.

Hash matches and abuse controls can automatically withhold content or flag accounts. An administrator reviews contested decisions; we do not describe an unreviewed permanent account ban as a verified automated legal finding. See [moderation and appeals](/moderation-policy). For a breach we assess harm, notify the relevant authority and affected people when required, and explain protective steps.
