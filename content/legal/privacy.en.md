## Where information goes

The application and database run on the operator's Easypanel server hosted in Canada by technocloud.ca. Cloudflare provides network protection and delivery. Planned local-upload storage is Cloudflare R2; storage, backup and processing locations are listed below. Account mail provider and location: {{EMAIL_PROVIDER_AND_LOCATION}}. Backup location: {{BACKUP_PROVIDER_AND_LOCATION}}. Active media-safety processors and what each receives: {{SAFETY_PROCESSORS_AND_DATA}}.

After cutover the previous PieFed instance on box.feddit.com is kept stopped and restorable for 30 days, and its offline archive is kept at {{PIEFED_ARCHIVE_LOCATION}} until {{PIEFED_ARCHIVE_DELETE_DATE}}, which is the cutover date plus 30 days. On that date both the instance and the archive are deleted unless a legal hold applies. Backups taken before cutover follow the ordinary backup row in the retention schedule. Actor signing keys are kept only as long as the federation identity needs them.

ActivityPub sends public posts, profiles, comments and interaction events to independent servers around the world. Those operators decide how they process their copies and are not simply our contracted processors. Remote public content is cached locally as database objects. Deletion requests are sent to peers, but we cannot make every peer erase its copy.

All remote media, including link-preview thumbnails, is streamed at view time through our signed non-persisting proxy, not hotlinked or mirrored. It hides the visitor's IP from the remote media host during that fetch. It does not make visits anonymous: feddit.com and Cloudflare process connection information, and opening an external link exposes you to that site's practices. The proxy must not save response bodies to disk, logs, object storage or CDN cache. Short-lived request buffers and signed URLs are not a durable media cache.

Link-preview thumbnails are not stored. They are fetched through the same proxy when a post is viewed and are not kept. Nothing fetched from remote servers is stored until our safety scanning is live; that scanning applies before publication to local uploads and, later, to any stored copies. Local uploads, avatars and other stored derivatives stay disabled if that scan path is unavailable.

International processing and federation require assessment under GDPR Chapter V where applicable. Canada hosting alone does not establish a valid transfer mechanism for all recipients. Statutory evidence periods: [L] [18 USC 2258A(h)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A), [UK SI 2026/268 reg 8](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/268/made/data.html), [TCO Art 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32021R0784).

Legal holds are narrow, access-limited and reviewed, with a reason, scope and expiry. They do not leave removed content public. Intimate images are not retained for 24 months merely because a report exists. We retain only necessary incident records unless a specific preservation basis requires the material.

## Your choices and rights

Contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} for access, correction, a copy/export, deletion, restriction or objection. Where applicable, you can withdraw consent and request portability of qualifying data. We verify identity proportionately and do not require an identity document by default. Exports exclude other people's private information and illegal material. Where GDPR applies, we respond without undue delay and within one calendar month. A complex request may allow up to two further months; we explain the extension within the first month. You may complain to your relevant supervisory authority and seek a judicial remedy. [L] [GDPR Arts 12, 15–22, 77–79](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679). We offer the same request route to everyone.
