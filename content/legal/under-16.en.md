# Minimum age and age reviews

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

You must be at least 16 to create or hold a feddit.com account. The rule applies during the invite-only beta and after registration opens. An invitation or parental permission does not override it.

At sign-up we ask for a neutral age question and record a 16+ declaration and its timestamp. We block immediate retries after an under-16 response. A refused sign-up creates no account; a short retry marker is the only record, kept as the refused sign-up row of our [retention schedule](/privacy) sets out. During the beta we also use the known invitation chain and review credible signs of underage accounts. A declaration alone does not prove age. These are our own measures; [eSafety guidance](https://www.esafety.gov.au/about-us/industry-regulation/social-media-age-restrictions/faqs) says self-declared age alone is insufficient.

To report an account you reasonably believe belongs to someone under 16, use the Report action on its profile or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with its profile URL and your reason. Do not publicly identify or harass the person, investigate them elsewhere, or ask them to send identity documents.

An administrator reviews the report and context. Unsupported allegations or appearance alone do not establish age. Where credible evidence warrants it, we temporarily suspend access while checking. We explain the concern privately, give a review route, and request only the minimum useful information. We do not make government ID the only way to establish age. [Australian Online Safety Act s63DB](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2021A00076/latest/text).

If we conclude the person is under 16, we deactivate the account and offer a safe export of their own lawful data without restoring posting access. The account then follows the deletion grace period row of our [retention schedule](/privacy): deactivated for 30 days, then deleted unless a legal hold applies. The decision states the applicable row and any specific legal hold. We do not keep accounts active for a 30-day grace period. An under-13 report is escalated promptly for privacy review.

If we got it wrong, reply to the decision or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the case number and an explanation. We aim to review within seven days. The beta's single administrator reconsiders the evidence; this is not an independent second review. A successful review restores the account if it has not yet been deleted, unless another valid restriction applies. Age-review evidence is minimised and deleted when no longer needed; the outcome and necessary safety record follow the [privacy schedule](/privacy).

People aged 16 and 17 are still children for UK online-safety purposes. This age rule does not remove our responsibility to assess and protect younger users or visitors. [UK OSA s236(1)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/236) defines a child as a person under 18; the children's access assessment duty is in [s35](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/section/35).
