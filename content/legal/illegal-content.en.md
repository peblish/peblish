# Report illegal or harmful content

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

Use the Report action on a post, comment, profile or community, or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You do not need an account to email us. In immediate danger, contact emergency services where the person at risk is located; our reporting route is not an emergency service.

Choose child safety, intimate image, terrorism or serious violence, threats or harassment, copyright, fraud or other illegality, under-16 account, or another community-rule concern. An uncertain category does not stop a report.

Give the exact feddit.com URL or object identifier, why you think it is illegal or harmful, and any relevant country and context. For a formal EU illegal-content notice, include your name and email and a good-faith statement that the information and allegations are accurate and complete. Name and email are not required for notices concerning child sexual abuse/exploitation offences covered by Arts 3–7 of Directive 2011/93/EU. We also accept anonymous safety reports outside those formal requirements. [L] [DSA Art 16](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065).

Do not download, forward or attach abusive images to prove your report. A URL and explanation are enough to begin review. Use the separate [intimate-image removal process](/take-it-down) if you are depicted or authorised to act for that person. Use [copyright notices](/dmca) for copyright claims.

We record receipt, assess urgency, restrict access when needed, review the available facts and tell you the outcome and review route if you provide contact details. We may request missing context without postponing urgent protective action. We keep your identity restricted and do not normally share it with the reported person. We may have to disclose limited information under applicable law; copyright counter-notices have their own disclosure rules.

We address content accessible through our instance even if it originated elsewhere. We can block local access and request action from an originating server, but cannot guarantee removal elsewhere.

We refer apparent child exploitation and qualifying threats or violent material to appropriate authorities. We verify law-enforcement demands, preserve only justified evidence, disclose only what the applicable process permits or requires, and notify affected users where lawful and safe. We do not provide direct database access in response to a routine request. [L] [DSA Arts 9–10, 18](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065).
