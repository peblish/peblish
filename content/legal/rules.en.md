# Community rules

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}.

1. Be at least 16 to have an account. Do not share or sell invitations or evade a restriction.
2. Do not post child sexual abuse material, sexualised minors (including fictional or generated depictions), grooming or exploitation.
3. Do not share intimate images without consent, including synthetic images, or threaten to share them.
4. No pornography, fetish content, graphic self-harm, encouragement of suicide or self-harm, or gratuitous graphic violence. Supportive discussion and non-graphic reporting are welcome.
5. No threats, terrorist promotion, targeted harassment, hate directed at protected groups, doxxing or coercive behaviour.
6. No scams, malware, spam, illegal transactions or deliberate copyright infringement. Describe sensitive links honestly; linking is not a way around these rules.
7. Respect community topics and other people's privacy. Mark permitted sensitive material. Do not repost removed material to challenge a decision; use the appeal route.

These rules apply to content shown through federation too. Serious abuse may result in immediate removal or suspension. We consider context and explain decisions. Report with the Report action on a post, comment, profile or community, or write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Read [moderation and appeals](/moderation-policy), [intimate-image removal](/take-it-down) and [under-16 reviews](/under-16).

These are this instance's own rules and are broader than the minimum law. A rule breach is not automatically a criminal offence.
