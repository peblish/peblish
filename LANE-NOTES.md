## 4. Decisions and deviations from the brief

1. **(Deviation) Extra deliverables.** Beyond the four items: the missing-object job (the realistic route to the lemmy.world Dislike's target, see section 1), `refreshRemoteActor` and `undeleteRemoteActor` (a 410 now deletes automatically, so a way back is needed; FED-A's review note said to add it "if remote deletion needs it"), and retention for `inbox_raw` and `deliveries` (both hand-offs asked for it).
2. **What vouches for a fetched object.** The inbound pipeline stores nothing in a remote community unless that community announced it. A fetch by IRI has no Announce, so the store takes a `Vouch`: the community served the object from its own collection (backfill: `announcedBy` is the collection's URL), or announced an activity about it (missing-object job: the row's `actor_ap_id` must be a remote Group we hold, and its activity id is the `announceApId`). Core's `post.create` and `comment.create` policies then read it as a community Announce (`mod.announce_mismatch` for any other community).
3. **Embedded objects are trusted only from their own server.** A post inside a community's outbox is used as is only when its id is on the community's origin; any other is read from where it lives (the pipeline's `embedded.unverifiable` rule). The test has the community embed a forged copy of another server's post; the stored title is the one that server serves.
4. **Featured posts are stored, not pinned.** Pinning is `mod.pin`, which writes a modlog row each time; a backfill would write one for something no moderator did here, and repeats could not tell a pin from a re-read. The live `Add` on the featured collection (FED-B) still pins. Open: a community's pins are missing until its next `Add`.
5. **NSFW.** The inbound rule (remote `sensitive` or `nsfl` is refused, `content.nsfw_rejected`) is repeated in `object.ts` (one line). When ADM adds the setting it must change both places; FED-B could export the predicate from `handlers/_content.ts`.
6. **`deleteRemoteActor` takes an optional input** `{ removeContent?, cause? }` and returns `{ deleted, posts, comments }` (it returned `{ deleted }`). FED-B's call `deleteRemoteActor.within(t, grant)` is unchanged and still compiles; it passes no input, so **a Delete(Person) removes no content until root makes the handler edit in section 5(a)**. `removeContent` is ignored for a Group.
7. **Ledger rows** (`deletion_ledger`, the B06 drill). One `actor` row per deleted remote account, `reason: 'remote.actor_deleted'`, `subject_ap_id` the actor's id, `details: { remote: true, cause: 'Delete' | 'Tombstone', removeContent, posts, comments }`. With `removeContent` there is no row per post or comment: **the restore must delete every post and comment authored by `subject_id` when `details.removeContent` is true** (an account can have thousands). A single object gone at its source is a `post` or `comment` row, `reason: 'remote.object_gone'`. An undelete is an `actor` row `reason: 'remote.actor_undeleted'` after the deletion's, so a restore applying rows in id order ends with the actor alive; content an earlier removal deleted is not restored (it cannot be told from content the author deleted).
8. **Remote deletions emit** `post.deleted` and `comment.deleted` with `local: false` for each object removed with an account (one outbox row each), then `actor.deleted`.
9. **Dormancy, as built** (`dormancy` in `core/remote/dormant.ts`, figures as data; root to confirm). Candidate: an unbroken run of failed (or retrying) deliveries since the instance's last delivered row, the earliest at least 7 days old, the latest within 2 days (a peer we stopped sending to is not "failing"), and at least 3 of them. Dormant: a candidate that sent nothing we stored in the last 7 days (looked up in `inbox_raw` by the sender's actor, so it counts activities that were later skipped). Probe: `last_probe_at` is set when it goes dormant, so the first probe is a day later; after that the first `isDormant` question a day after the last probe answers "no" (that delivery is the probe) and every other answers "yes" (the row ends `skipped`); the claim is a mutation under the row lock, so concurrent delivery batches let exactly one through. A retry of the probe that failed is skipped too (the next probe is a day on). Revival needs evidence: a delivered row after `dormant_since`, or an inbound row from the instance after it. Absence from the candidate list does not revive it, because pruning removes the failed rows. Inbound revival is by the health job (every 10 minutes), not immediate.
10. **`isDormant(instanceId, now = new Date())`** gained a `now` for tests; the delivery worker calls it with one argument as before. It reads one primary-key row per delivery; only a dormant instance costs a mutation.
11. **Retention** (`retention` in `core/remote/prune.ts`): settled inbox rows 14 days, settled deliveries 30 days, 2,000 rows a transaction and 25 transactions a run, hourly. A pending row is never deleted. An `activities` row loses its `inbox_raw_id` (ON DELETE SET NULL), and the relay then falls back to the parsed copy, as the table's comment says. Pruning inbox rows also removes the `target.missing` rows the missing-object job reads; it looks back 7 days, inside the window.
12. **Backfill caps** (`backfillLimits`, data): 50 posts, 100 comments a post and 500 a community, 3 pages and 100 items a collection, 250 requests, a 15 minute lease, two retries (after 5 minutes and an hour) then `failed`. A cut-short run settles `done` with a note (`backfills.note`).
13. **Politeness is per process** (`polite.ts` keeps its queues on `globalThis`): one request at a time to a host with a 300 ms gap, for the backfill and the missing-object job. FED-A's delivery and the inbound pipeline's own fetches do not go through it. One worker runs the jobs, so this is one at a time for the instance; two workers would each keep their own.
14. **The missing-object job looks at the newest waiting activities first** (25 objects a run), skips an object on this server's own origin (we never fetch ourselves), and **remembers in memory** (per process, `globalThis`) which objects it asked for and when: a down peer is asked again after 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 12 h, 24 h; a 410, a refusal or a stored object is not asked for again until the process restarts (one more request per still-missing object then). It never settles `inbox_raw` rows itself.
15. **A comment's missing ancestors** are fetched up to 5 levels (`maxParentDepth`), one request each; a longer chain refuses the comment as `parent.missing`.
16. **Jobs and consumer names:** consumer `federation.backfill`; jobs `federation.backfill_run`, `federation.missing_objects`, `remote.instance_health`, `remote.prune`. The consumer declares `startAt: 'beginning'`, so every `follow.accepted` already in the outbox queues its community's backfill at the first deploy.
17. **Both new tables are in `core/src/remote/tables.ts`**; the inherited `actors.ts` and `instances.ts` changed only as the brief says (`isDormant` is now implemented in `dormant.ts` and re-exported from `instances.ts`, so FED-A's import path is unchanged).

**SAFE**

- Backfill stores peer URLs only (link, thumbnail, icon); it fetches no media. Nothing it stores is rendered unsanitised: bodies go through `remoteBody` as for the live path, and pages still pass them through the media-proxy renderer.
- Peer JSON is kept in more places than `inbox_raw`: `actors.raw`, `posts.raw`, `activities.raw`. Retention prunes only `inbox_raw` (14 days) and `deliveries` (30 days). A takedown that must erase a peer's document has to clear those too.
- Backfill and the missing-object job are the first code that fetches peer objects the peer did not send us in a signed POST. Every fetch goes through `safeFetch` (https only, no redirect to a private address, 512 KiB cap) and is bounded (caps in section 4 item 12, 25 objects a minute). A hostile community can make us read up to 50 posts and 250 requests per follow.
- A hostile community cannot put a post in another community, author as another server's user (the object is read from the author's server unless it is on the community's own), or store sensitive content (refused as inbound).
- `deletion_ledger` rows for remote deletions are described in section 4 item 7; the B06 drill must apply `details.removeContent` and the undelete rows.

## 9. Left open and risks

- Section 5(a) is required for Lemmy's "delete my content"; until it is made a Delete(Person) removes no content. Section 5(b) and (c) are small and recommended.
- Featured posts are stored but not pinned until the community's next `Add` (section 4 item 4).
- No comments from Lemmy or PieFed in a backfill (they publish none); a thread arrives through the live Announces we receive from the follow onward, and through `missing.ts` when something names a comment.
- The collection shapes are not captured from a real Lemmy or PieFed (the corpus has none); LAB checks them.
